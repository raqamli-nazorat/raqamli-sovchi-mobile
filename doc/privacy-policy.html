Raqamli Sovchi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-04 Developer: Raqamli Nazorat LLC Contact: support@raqamlisovchi.uz

Raqamli Sovchi is a serious marriage-focused social platform. The product helps bride, groom, and representative users find suitable candidates through profile information, compatibility questionnaires, privacy controls, and moderation.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Raqamli Sovchi mobile application, backend services, account registration, authentication, profile, questionnaire, matching, communication, moderation, and account deletion features when they are enabled.

If a feature is not yet enabled in the app, the related data is not collected until the feature is released and used.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and authentication information

  • phone number;
  • email address, if email sign-in is enabled;
  • Telegram authentication session data, if Telegram sign-in is used;
  • Google sign-in data, if Google sign-in is enabled;
  • OneID or JSHSHIR-related identity data, if OneID integration is enabled in the future;
  • access token, refresh token, and session metadata needed to keep you signed in.

Authentication tokens and sensitive local session values are stored securely on the device where possible.

2.2 Local PIN and biometric unlock

The app may let you create a 4-digit local PIN. The PIN is stored only in secure local device storage and is used to unlock the app on the same device.

If biometric unlock is enabled, biometric verification is handled by the device operating system. Raqamli Sovchi does not receive or store your fingerprint, face, or other biometric template.

2.3 Profile information

For bride or groom users, we may collect first name, last name, father's name, birth year, height, weight, region, district, health or disability status, marital status, candidate type, optional profile photos, optional text description, optional voice introduction, and optional location information.

For representative users, we may collect representative information, relationship to the candidate, and candidate profile information such as name, birth year, height, weight, health or disability status, marital status, region, district, optional photos, optional text description, optional voice introduction, and optional location information.

2.4 Compatibility questionnaire information

If you complete the compatibility questionnaire, we may collect answers about religious and spiritual values, family management, financial views, work and education expectations, relatives and mother-in-law/daughter-in-law relationship expectations, conflict handling, psychological compatibility, future family plans, children, parenting, health, lifestyle, and personal development.

2.5 Photos, audio, chat, and media

If related features are enabled, we may collect profile photos, short voice introductions, chat messages, voice notes, video or other media attachments, and moderation signals connected to content reports or abuse prevention.

2.6 Device, network, and technical information

  • device type and operating system version;
  • app version;
  • crash and error information;
  • IP address and request metadata;
  • push notification token, if notifications are enabled;
  • security and abuse-prevention logs.

We do not intentionally log phone numbers, tokens, private chat content, profile photos, or other sensitive personal content in release logs.

3. How we use information

  • to create and authenticate user accounts;
  • to restore sessions and protect accounts with local PIN or biometric unlock;
  • to create bride, groom, or representative profiles;
  • to verify serious marriage-focused intent;
  • to recommend compatible candidates;
  • to support optional AI-based profile and questionnaire matching;
  • to show or hide profile photos according to privacy settings;
  • to enable communication features when available;
  • to detect spam, abuse, inappropriate messages, or harmful behavior;
  • to process reports, blocks, and moderation actions;
  • to provide customer support;
  • to improve app stability and security;
  • to comply with legal and platform policy obligations.

We do not sell user personal data.

4. AI-based processing

If you agree to AI-assisted matching, your profile and questionnaire answers may be processed to suggest compatible candidates. AI suggestions are support tools only and do not guarantee marriage compatibility or relationship outcomes.

5. Privacy and visibility controls

Raqamli Sovchi is designed for privacy-first matchmaking.

  • blurred or hidden profile photos;
  • profile visibility only for approved or compatible users;
  • screenshot protection;
  • no public search engine indexing of user profiles;
  • report and block tools;
  • moderation for inappropriate behavior or content.

Screenshot protection reduces in-app screenshots but cannot prevent someone from recording the screen with another camera or external device.

6. Sharing of information

We may share information only when needed for backend hosting, authentication providers, push notification services, crash reporting or operational monitoring, moderation, legal compliance, fraud prevention, security review, or service providers acting on behalf of Raqamli Sovchi.

We do not allow service providers to use personal data for their own independent marketing.

7. Legal, safety, and moderation disclosures

We may access, preserve, or disclose information if necessary to comply with applicable law, investigate abuse, protect users or Raqamli Sovchi, enforce app rules, or respond to reports and moderation requests.

8. Data retention

We keep account data while your account is active. After account deletion, we delete or de-identify account data where deletion is allowed. Some records may be retained for a limited period when required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, abuse investigation, dispute resolution, accounting, or operational records.

Data retained for these reasons is not used for normal profile matching.

9. Account deletion

  1. Open the Raqamli Sovchi app.
  2. Sign in.
  3. Open the home or settings area.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm deletion.

If you cannot access the app, send an account deletion request to support@raqamlisovchi.uz. Include the phone number, email address, or other account identifier connected to your Raqamli Sovchi account so we can verify ownership.

Account deletion web page in this repository: doc/index.html.

10. Your choices and rights

  • access your account information in the app;
  • edit profile information;
  • remove optional photos, descriptions, or voice introductions;
  • disable optional permissions from device settings;
  • request account deletion;
  • contact support about data access, correction, or deletion.

11. Permissions

The app asks for permissions only when needed. Possible permissions include biometric, notification, photo or media access, microphone, camera, and location permissions.

12. Children and age suitability

Raqamli Sovchi is intended for serious marriage-focused use and is not intended for children. Users must meet the minimum legal and product age requirements applicable to their country or region.

13. Security

We use safeguards designed to protect user information, including HTTPS, secure local storage for sensitive tokens and local PIN data, screenshot protection where supported, backend authorization checks, moderation and abuse-prevention controls, and limited logging of sensitive information.

14. International use

Raqamli Sovchi is primarily designed for users connected to Uzbekistan and Uzbek-speaking communities. If the app becomes available outside Uzbekistan, data may be processed through infrastructure or providers located in other countries, depending on hosting and service configuration.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, backend, legal requirements, or platform policies change. When material changes are made, we will update the effective date and may notify users through the app or another appropriate channel.
