Raqamli Sovchi account deletion

This page explains how Raqamli Sovchi users can delete their account and request deletion of associated app data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Raqamli Sovchi mobile app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open the home/settings area.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

After confirmation, your account deletion request is sent to the Raqamli Sovchi backend and your local app session is cleared.

If you cannot access the app

If you deleted the app or cannot sign in, send an account deletion request by email. Include the phone number or email address connected to your Raqamli Sovchi account so we can identify it.

Request account deletion

What data is deleted

  • Your app account.
  • Your profile data connected to the account.
  • Authentication/session data connected to the account.
  • Other app data associated with the account where deletion is allowed.

Data we may retain

Some records may be retained only when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, or accounting obligations. Data that is retained for these reasons is not used for normal app profile matching.

Processing time

In-app deletion is processed immediately when the backend accepts the request. Email deletion requests are reviewed and processed within 30 days after we verify account ownership.

Raqamli Sovchi hisobini o'chirish

Ushbu sahifada Raqamli Sovchi foydalanuvchilari hisobini va unga bog'liq ilova ma'lumotlarini qanday o'chirish mumkinligi tushuntiriladi.

Ilova ichida hisobni o'chirish

  1. Raqamli Sovchi mobil ilovasini oching.
  2. Hisobingizga kiring.
  3. Bosh sahifa yoki sozlamalar bo'limini oching.
  4. Hisobni o'chirish tugmasini bosing.
  5. O'chirish so'rovini tasdiqlang.

Ilovaga kira olmasangiz

Ilovani o'chirib yuborgan bo'lsangiz yoki hisobga kira olmasangiz, email orqali so'rov yuboring. Hisobingizni aniqlashimiz uchun Raqamli Sovchi hisobiga bog'langan telefon raqam yoki email manzilni kiriting.
