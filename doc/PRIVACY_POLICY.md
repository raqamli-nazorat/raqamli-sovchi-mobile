# Raqamli Sovchi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-04

Developer: Raqamli Nazorat LLC

Contact: support@raqamlisovchi.uz

This Privacy Policy explains how the Raqamli Sovchi mobile application collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes user information.

Raqamli Sovchi is a serious marriage-focused social platform. The product goal is to help bride, groom, and representative users find suitable candidates through profile information, compatibility questionnaires, privacy controls, and moderation.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to:

- the Raqamli Sovchi mobile application;
- Raqamli Sovchi backend services;
- account registration and authentication flows;
- profile, questionnaire, matching, communication, moderation, and account deletion features when they are enabled.

If a feature is not yet enabled in the app, the related data is not collected until the feature is released and used.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account and authentication information

We may collect:

- phone number;
- email address, if email sign-in is enabled;
- Telegram authentication session data, if Telegram sign-in is used;
- Google sign-in data, if Google sign-in is enabled;
- OneID or JSHSHIR-related identity data, if OneID integration is enabled in the future;
- access token, refresh token, and session metadata needed to keep you signed in.

Authentication tokens and sensitive local session values are stored securely on the device where possible.

### 2.2 Local PIN and biometric unlock

The app may let you create a 4-digit local PIN. The PIN is stored only in secure local device storage and is used to unlock the app on the same device.

If biometric unlock is enabled, biometric verification is handled by the device operating system. Raqamli Sovchi does not receive or store your fingerprint, face, or other biometric template.

### 2.3 Profile information

Depending on your user type, we may collect profile information.

For bride or groom users, this may include:

- first name, last name, and father's name;
- birth year;
- height and weight;
- region and district;
- health or disability status;
- marital status;
- candidate type;
- optional profile photos;
- optional text description about yourself;
- optional voice introduction;
- optional location permission and location information.

For representative users, this may include:

- representative first name and last name;
- relationship to the candidate;
- candidate type;
- candidate first name and last name;
- candidate birth year;
- candidate height and weight;
- candidate health or disability status;
- candidate marital status;
- candidate region and district;
- optional candidate photos;
- optional candidate text description;
- optional candidate voice introduction;
- optional location permission and location information.

### 2.4 Compatibility questionnaire information

If you choose to complete the compatibility questionnaire, we may collect your answers about:

- religious and spiritual values;
- family management views;
- financial views;
- expectations about work, education, and household responsibilities;
- relatives and mother-in-law/daughter-in-law relationship expectations;
- conflict handling and psychological compatibility;
- future family plans;
- children and parenting views;
- health, lifestyle, and personal development views.

Some questionnaire questions may be used to detect inconsistent or unrealistic answers and improve matching quality.

### 2.5 Photos, audio, chat, and media

If related features are enabled, we may collect:

- profile photos;
- short voice introductions;
- chat messages;
- voice notes;
- video or other media attachments;
- moderation signals connected to content reports or abuse prevention.

Photos, audio, chat, and media may contain sensitive personal information. The app is designed to use privacy controls, profile visibility settings, screenshot protection, and moderation to reduce misuse.

### 2.6 Device, network, and technical information

We may collect limited technical information needed for security, reliability, and app functionality:

- device type and operating system version;
- app version;
- crash and error information;
- IP address and request metadata;
- push notification token, if notifications are enabled;
- security and abuse-prevention logs.

We do not intentionally log phone numbers, tokens, private chat content, profile photos, or other sensitive personal content in release logs.

## 3. How we use information

We use collected information to:

- create and authenticate user accounts;
- restore sessions and protect accounts with local PIN or biometric unlock;
- create bride, groom, or representative profiles;
- verify user intent for serious marriage-focused use;
- recommend compatible candidates;
- support optional AI-based profile and questionnaire matching;
- show or hide profile photos according to privacy settings;
- enable communication features when available;
- detect spam, abuse, inappropriate messages, or harmful behavior;
- process reports, blocks, and moderation actions;
- provide customer support;
- improve app stability and security;
- comply with legal and platform policy obligations.

We do not sell user personal data.

## 4. AI-based processing

If you agree to AI-assisted matching, your profile and questionnaire answers may be processed to suggest compatible candidates.

AI processing may use information such as values, family expectations, location preferences, profile details, and questionnaire answers. AI suggestions are only support tools and do not guarantee marriage compatibility or relationship outcomes.

Users should provide honest answers and make final decisions themselves, with family involvement where appropriate.

## 5. Privacy and visibility controls

Raqamli Sovchi is designed for privacy-first matchmaking.

The app may provide:

- blurred or hidden profile photos;
- profile visibility only for approved or compatible users;
- screenshot protection;
- no public search engine indexing of user profiles;
- report and block tools;
- moderation for inappropriate behavior or content.

Screenshot protection reduces in-app screenshots but cannot prevent someone from recording the screen with another camera or external device.

## 6. Sharing of information

We may share information only when needed for:

- backend hosting and infrastructure;
- authentication providers, such as Telegram, Google, email, phone, or OneID, when used;
- push notification services, if enabled;
- crash reporting or operational monitoring, if enabled;
- moderation, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or security review;
- service providers acting on behalf of Raqamli Sovchi.

We do not allow service providers to use personal data for their own independent marketing.

## 7. Legal, safety, and moderation disclosures

We may access, preserve, or disclose information if necessary to:

- comply with applicable law or official requests;
- investigate abuse, fraud, harassment, or policy violations;
- protect users, the public, or Raqamli Sovchi;
- enforce app rules, terms, or community guidelines;
- respond to reports and moderation requests.

## 8. Data retention

We keep account data while your account is active.

After account deletion, we delete or de-identify account data where deletion is allowed. Some records may be retained for a limited period when required for:

- legal obligations;
- security and fraud prevention;
- abuse investigation;
- dispute resolution;
- accounting or operational records.

Data retained for these reasons is not used for normal profile matching.

## 9. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app:

1. Open the Raqamli Sovchi app.
2. Sign in.
3. Open the home or settings area.
4. Tap **Delete account**.
5. Confirm deletion.

If you cannot access the app, you may send an account deletion request to:

support@raqamlisovchi.uz

Include the phone number, email address, or other account identifier connected to your Raqamli Sovchi account so we can verify ownership.

Account deletion web page:

`doc/index.html`

For Google Play, this page should be deployed to a public URL such as:

`https://raqamlisovchi.uz/account-deletion`

or a GitHub Pages URL.

## 10. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and product availability, you may:

- access your account information in the app;
- edit profile information;
- remove optional photos, descriptions, or voice introductions;
- disable optional permissions from device settings;
- request account deletion;
- contact support about data access, correction, or deletion.

## 11. Permissions

The app asks for permissions only when needed.

Possible permissions include:

- biometric permission for local unlock;
- notification permission for app notifications;
- photo or media access for profile photos and uploads;
- microphone access for voice introduction or voice notes;
- camera access for profile or media features;
- location permission for location-based matching, if enabled.

If you deny a permission, some features may not work, but the app should continue to handle the denial safely.

## 12. Children and age suitability

Raqamli Sovchi is intended for serious marriage-focused use and is not intended for children.

Users must meet the minimum legal and product age requirements applicable to their country or region. If we learn that a child has provided personal information without proper authorization, we will take steps to delete that information.

## 13. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information, including:

- HTTPS for network communication;
- secure local storage for sensitive tokens and local PIN data;
- screenshot protection where supported;
- backend authorization checks;
- moderation and abuse-prevention controls;
- limited logging of sensitive information.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their device, PIN, Telegram account, Google account, email, and phone number.

## 14. International use

Raqamli Sovchi is primarily designed for users connected to Uzbekistan and Uzbek-speaking communities. If the app becomes available outside Uzbekistan, data may be processed through infrastructure or providers located in other countries, depending on hosting and service configuration.

## 15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, backend, legal requirements, or platform policies change.

When material changes are made, we will update the effective date and may notify users through the app or another appropriate channel.
