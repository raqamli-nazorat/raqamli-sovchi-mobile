Raqamli Sovchi Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 2026-08-04 Developer: Raqamli Nazorat LLC Contact: raqamlinazorat3@gmail.com

Raqamli Sovchi is a serious marriage-focused social platform. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation, harmful behavior toward minors, and any use of the platform that places children at risk.

Raqamli Sovchi has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, solicitation of minors, sexualized content involving minors, and any behavior that endangers children.

1. Scope

These Child Safety Standards apply to the Raqamli Sovchi mobile application, related backend services, user profiles, messages, media, moderation workflows, and support operations connected to the app.

2. Age Restrictions

Raqamli Sovchi is intended for adults using the platform for serious, marriage-focused matchmaking. The app is not intended for children. Users must satisfy the minimum legal age required by applicable law and by the product rules of the platform.

3. Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on Raqamli Sovchi:

  • child sexual abuse material or any exploitative material involving minors;
  • grooming, coercion, enticement, or sexual solicitation of minors;
  • sexualized conversations or requests directed at minors;
  • attempts to move minors to private or off-platform contact for exploitation;
  • sharing, requesting, storing, or promoting exploitative content involving minors;
  • impersonation, trafficking-related behavior, blackmail, threats, or harassment involving minors;
  • any other conduct that endangers children or violates applicable child safety laws.

4. Detection, Reporting, and Enforcement

We take reports of child safety concerns seriously. Where available, users may use in-app reporting and blocking tools. Users may also contact us directly at raqamlinazorat3@gmail.com to report child safety concerns, exploitative behavior, suspicious accounts, or harmful content.

When we identify or receive credible information about prohibited conduct, we may take action including:

  • removing content;
  • suspending or terminating accounts;
  • restricting access to platform features;
  • preserving relevant records where legally required;
  • reporting to appropriate authorities, child protection organizations, or platform partners when necessary.

5. Moderation Approach

Raqamli Sovchi may use a combination of human review, rule-based controls, account restrictions, and other moderation measures to reduce harmful conduct on the platform. Safety-related review may include profile information, messages, media, abuse reports, and account behavior where necessary to investigate a policy violation.

6. Cooperation with Law Enforcement and Child Protection Requests

We may preserve, review, and disclose relevant information where required by law, where necessary to investigate credible child safety concerns, or where necessary to protect children, users, or the platform. We cooperate with valid legal requests and may escalate matters to appropriate authorities.

7. User Responsibilities

Users of Raqamli Sovchi must:

  • use the service lawfully and respectfully;
  • avoid any conduct that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors;
  • report suspicious behavior, suspicious profiles, or harmful content promptly;
  • avoid sharing or requesting unlawful, exploitative, or abusive material.

8. Related Policies

These standards work together with our privacy and account policies. For more information about data handling, see the Raqamli Sovchi Privacy Policy.
